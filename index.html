The thesis

The consulting model is the thing AI kills first.

Every "AI for legal" consultancy sells you the same product: bodies and weeks. A diagnostic that takes a month. An implementation team that embeds for a quarter. A deck at the end. You are paying professional-services rates for work that AI now does in days — sold to you by people advising you to adopt AI.

The irony is the opportunity. When the analysis, the drafting, the policy work, and the workflow builds are AI-run, what you're actually buying is judgment: knowing which answer is right for a law firm, which workflow survives partner pushback, and which vendor is selling vapor.

So MatterValue is built the way we tell firms to build: AI does the labor, the operator supplies the judgment, and the price is fixed. A diagnostic in five business days, not two weeks of hallway interviews. Governance packs and prompt libraries as products, not billable projects. Advisory as a standing relationship, not a proposal cycle.

One operator who has sat in the partnership meeting, carried the P&L, signed the vendor checks, and runs a multi-state firm right now. That doesn't scale by hiring associates. It scales by productizing — which is exactly what your firm should be doing too. Consider this site the demo.
